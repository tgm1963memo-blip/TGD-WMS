# SOP: Transfer, Adjustment, And Stock Count

## Internal Transfer

### Purpose

Internal transfer moves customer-owned inventory from one warehouse/location/pallet context to another without changing ownership.

### Steps

1. Identify stock by customer, product, lot, pallet, and source location.
2. Create or open transfer document.
3. Enter source and target warehouse/location.
4. Verify physical movement.
5. Confirm transfer only through the approved UAT workflow.
6. Verify stock balance by location.
7. Verify movement ledger.

## Inventory Adjustment

### Purpose

Inventory adjustment corrects approved stock differences. Adjustments must be controlled and traceable.

### Steps

1. Identify product, customer, lot, pallet, and location.
2. Select adjustment type and reason.
3. Enter adjustment quantity and UOM.
4. Attach remark or evidence where required.
5. Obtain warehouse manager review according to UAT role assumption.
6. Confirm adjustment only through approved adjustment workflow.
7. Verify stock balance and movement ledger.

## Stock Count / Cycle Count

### Purpose

Stock count verifies physical customer-owned inventory against system balance.

### Steps

1. Create or open stock count document.
2. Select warehouse/location/product/lot/pallet scope.
3. Count physical quantity.
4. Record counted quantity.
5. Compare counted quantity to expected quantity.
6. Review variance with warehouse manager.
7. Create adjustment draft if applicable and approved by the current UAT process.
8. Verify final stock only after approved adjustment posting.

## Variance Review

- Positive variance: physical count is greater than system quantity.
- Negative variance: physical count is less than system quantity.
- Zero variance: physical count matches system quantity.
- All variance must include reason or evidence.

## Movement Ledger Verification

- Transfer: verify source and target locations.
- Adjustment: verify direction and reason.
- Stock count adjustment: verify reference to count or adjustment document where available.

## Approval Assumptions

- Warehouse manager reviews transfer exceptions, adjustment reasons, and stock count variance.
- Accounting is consulted when variance affects billing or customer dispute.
- Final approval rules depend on the approved UAT role model.

## Common Mistakes

- Selecting wrong customer-owned stock.
- Moving the wrong pallet or lot.
- Adjusting stock without evidence.
- Entering quantity in wrong UOM.
- Treating count variance as final before approval.

## Control Points

- No stock correction without documented reason.
- No physical movement without system traceability.
- Movement ledger must match approved operation.
- Customer ownership must remain unchanged unless separately approved outside this SOP.

## Evidence / Record-keeping

- Keep transfer, adjustment, stock count, and variance transaction reference numbers.
- Capture screenshot or evidence before and after transfer, adjustment, or count completion.
- Record movement ledger reference for transfer and adjustment impacts.
- Record stock balance evidence before and after approved stock-impacting operations.
- Record operator name, timestamp, variance reason, and reviewer/approver where applicable.
