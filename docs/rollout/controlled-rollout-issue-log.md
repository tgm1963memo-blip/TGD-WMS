## Severity Guide

| Severity | Meaning | Expected handling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical | Blocks controlled operation or creates security/stock trust risk | Escalate immediately; consider rollback |
| High | Major workflow/report issue with business impact | Assign owner same day; review workaround |
| Medium | Issue has workaround or limited impact | Track and schedule fix |
| Low | Cosmetic or improvement item | Add to backlog |
