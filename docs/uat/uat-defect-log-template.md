## Severity Guide

- Critical: Blocks UAT or risks incorrect stock, movement, permission, or accounting review outcome.
- High: Blocks a required operation or accounting review flow without workaround.
- Medium: Workaround exists or impact is limited.
- Low: Cosmetic, wording, or minor usability issue.
