## Access Levels
- **read** – View‑only access.
- **write** – Create or modify data.
- **review** – Read‑only with additional annotation capability (used in accounting review).
- **admin** – Full access, including configuration changes.

## Route Access Boundary Overview
- **Warehouse Staff** can operate within the *receiving*, *putaway*, *transfer*, *withdrawal*, *allocation*, *picking*, and *dispatch* areas (read/write where appropriate).
- **Warehouse Manager** has read/write rights on *master_data*, *adjustment*, *stock_count*, and may also act as staff for operational areas.
- **Accounting** is limited to the *accounting_review* area (read‑only preview of charge summaries).
- **Viewer** role can view any reports but cannot modify data.
- **Admin** has unrestricted access across all areas, including legacy placeholder routes.
